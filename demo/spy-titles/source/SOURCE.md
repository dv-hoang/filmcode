# DEAD DROP

A 60s cut-paper spy title sequence in the aesthetic tradition of Saul Bass and Kuntzel + Deygas. Flat opaque inks (red, mustard, black, off-white paper), razor-sharp silhouettes, dossier redactions, and bold typography.
