# Source

- **Story**: original concept of a mechanical gear design brought to life on a blueprint sheet.
- **Visuals**: Canvas 2D drawing, Prussian blue background, white line work, single red APPROVED stamp.
- **Audio**: simple 220 Hz sine tone, 10 s, generated with Python.
- **Build**: `source/build.sh` renders a blue video and muxes the audio via ffmpeg.
