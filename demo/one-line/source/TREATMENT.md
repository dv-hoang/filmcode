# The Mend — Treatment

**Style:** One-line Drawing (`one-line`)
**Length:** 40.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps (on ones)
**Language:** English, Kokoro `af_heart`
**Brief:** Original showcase. No storyboard pause. Not a remake of the library card.

One continuous ink line on warm paper. The line is a weaver’s thread. It never lifts.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Snapped thread, then the blanket.** A spool unwinds into a weft; the thread fatigues and stops; a smaller hand catches the end; one gold mend; a pull-back shows the whole night was one blanket, and the thread runs off the frame. *Chosen.*
2. **The thread as a graph.** Tension over one night: each peak is a pass of the shuttle, the trough is the break. *Rejected:* the chart would steal the handoff, and the final picture would read as data, not a thing someone sleeps under.
3. **A loop home.** The thread sews out and retraces to the spool, which now means “finished.” *Rejected:* retracing the whole journey spends the runtime twice and buries the stop.

**Opening:** mid-line at speed. The inner spiral is already inked; we join the nib as it leaves the spool. No tap, no logo, no empty paper.
**Ending:** the border closes, the camera locks, the line runs off the right edge and the scratch keeps going after the picture has let it go.
**Score shape:** one solo violin, one melodic line. Short notes while the shuttle travels, notes lengthening as the thread thins, one long note dying into a hard silence on the snap, a single pizzicato as the only second colour when the hand changes, the highest arco note on the gold mend, then a walking line around the border that thins away as the thread leaves.

---

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** A weaver’s thread snaps on the last pass; a smaller hand catches the end, and the only gold in the night is the mend — which, pulled back, is the seam of a blanket.

**Arc:**
- **Setup (0–8 s):** The nib is already running. A spiral reads as a spool; the line becomes a weft travelling right.
- **Turn (8–16 s):** The line dries and trembles, crawls, and stops. Ink pools in a teardrop. Music and room tone cut to nothing.
- **Ending (16–40 s):** Another hand’s shadow takes the pen. A slow gold seam repairs the break. The same line walks the blanket’s border. The wide frame shows spool, weft, blot and mend were one cloth. The thread leaves the frame.

---

## 3. Benchmark

Grammar only, from the style note: Cavandoli (one line is both world and character; a transformation is the transition), Mili’s Picasso light drawings (speed and confidence of a single gesture), McLaren (the line drawn *to* the notes).

| Learn | Don’t take |
|---|---|
| Continuous morph instead of a cut; speed as emotion; a stop that the music also obeys; the camera lagging the nib | La Linea’s character, gibberish voice, any existing continuous-line illustration, any library demo’s story |

## 4. Shot list

No cuts. One camera phrase: centre, zoom, a few degrees of roll, follow weight. The follow point averages the nib over about −0.55…+0.4 s, clamped into the central 70% of the frame.

| # | Framing | Angle | Move | Dur | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Macro | top-down on the paper | Follow the nib, slight roll with the spiral | 0–3 s | Join a gesture already in motion; the spool must read before the journey |
| 2 | Medium | top-down | Follow, high weight | 3–8 s | The spiral’s exit *is* the first weft wave — the morph is the joke and the plot |
| 3 | Medium-wide | top-down | Lateral, line running ahead of a lower follow weight | 8–12.5 s | Horizon of the cloth; fatigue has room to show |
| 4 | Close | top-down | Slow push-in, follow falling toward a hold | 12.5–16 s | The stop is the event; the blot has to be large enough to read as a knot, not a speck |
| 5 | Close | top-down | Roll with the new hand, follow returns | 16–19.5 s | Handoff: two shadows, one layer each, the pen changes voice |
| 6 | Medium | top-down | Composed on the seam, low-moderate follow | 19.5–23.8 s | The gold mend is the slowest stroke and must sit clear of the weft |
| 7 | Wide | top-down | Continuous pull-back as the border is walked | 23.8–33 s | Signature: every earlier vignette is a region of one blanket |
| 8 | Wide, locked | top-down | Hold; follow near zero | 33–40 s | The line runs off without the camera chasing it; title sits in empty paper above |

**Signature shot:** #7 — the pull-back. The spool, the waves, the blot and the gold seam are one blanket, drawn by the border that is still the same line.

**Moves used:** nib-follow, lateral lead, push-in hold, roll, pull-back, locked wide. More than four. No cut, no fade.

## 6. Cue map

**Tempo:** 60 BPM throughout, 4/4. The picture ritards; the grid does not. Ten bars, 40.0 s.

**Instrument:** solo violin arco, one note at a time. One pizzicato note at 16.0 only (the handoff). No pad, no second line.

| Bars | Time | Violin | Picture hit on the beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | Moving eighths, D dorian, mid register (A3–E4) | 0.0 nib already running; 2.0 voice 1 |

Corners of the path (speed minima at high turn) are candidate note onsets: spiral exit, each wave crest, the stop, the gold’s two bends, the four border corners. The score uses those, not a separate melody dropped on top.

## 7. Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 spool and weft | very light paper room, low | pen scratch bright, fibre ticks ∝ speed, pan follows nib screen-x | arco travelling |
| 8–14 fatigue | room thins | scratch duller, broken by dry gaps, amplitude ∝ √speed | notes lengthen, level falls |
| 14–16 stop | **room tone off** | wet blot swell 14.0–14.5, then nothing | **hard zero, tails included** |
| 16–19.5 handoff | room back, lighter | tiny rub at 16.0; young pen, lighter and jerkier | one pizzicato, then short arco |
| 19.5–23.8 mend | quiet room | slow dull scratch, almost no ticks | highest arco note, then down |
| 23.8–34 border | room steady | scratch returns toward travelling, pan walks the frame | walking quarters |
| 34–40 exit | room fades with the pen | scratch pans off the right and dies | last note already gone |

Music ducks about 5 dB under voice. Pen ducks about 4 dB under voice. Master −14 LUFS. Grain 0.

Two silences: the near-silence while the line crawls (about 11–14 s), and the literal cut (14–16 s). The first sound after the cut is the handoff rub.

Sound as transition: the young pen’s lighter ticks begin a fraction before the new shadow is fully there (J into the handoff). The scratch continues past the frame edge after the camera has stopped following (L out of the picture).

## 8. Subtitles and title

**Subtitles.** Caveat, ink at 84% of `#1D1A17`, no box, centred near the bottom. Written left to right (0.2 s + about 17 ms per character) over a soft paper-coloured halo (`#F4EFE4`) so a passing line never cuts a letter. Fade about 0.45 s. Hold at least max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).

| id | t0 write | speech | text |
|---|---|---|---|
| v01 | 1.75 | 2.00 | One thread held the night. |
| v02 | 16.15 | 16.35 | A smaller hand caught the end. |
| v03 | 20.00 | 20.20 | Only the mend was gold. |
| v04 | 27.75 | 28.00 | The whole night was a blanket. |

Windows are retimed to the real Kokoro durations before the mix, and the `.srt` uses those same windows.

**Title.** “The Mend”, Sacramento (monoline script, itself one line), written on from 32.6 s in the empty margin above the blanket, after the camera has locked, so the camera never travels into the letters. Hold through 40.0 s (write plus hold > 4 s). No credit line on the picture.

**Accent.** One gold stretch, `#A9782C`, on the mend only. It returns to `#1D1A17` and stays gold in the finished blanket. No second accent.

**Line voices.** Confident medium nib on the spool; tremble and dry-brush through the fatigue; teardrop blot at the stop; lighter, jerkier young hand after the handoff; slowest base on the gold; steady again on the border.

## Picture plan (for the path, not a shot from anywhere else)

World origin at the blanket’s centre. Final motif, drawn once:

- Inner spiral, upper-left of the field (the spool).
- Four weft waves across the field, gap well above the ink width.
- Stop and teardrop on the last crest’s descent.
- Short young-hand bridge, then the gold darn, clear of the waves.
- Rounded rectangle border walked once; the gold arrives on the right side and the walk leaves where it arrived, then continues off the right edge.

Connectors are those joins. Nothing extra. A debug view of the whole path at the final zoom is the test that the reveal has no stray wrinkle.
