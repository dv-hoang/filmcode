# Microgame Frenzy

Fast-paced, multi-medium microgame anthology celebrating rapid-fire gameplay art styles (blueprint, risograph, retro terminal, arcade cabinet).

- **Visual Style:** Multi-medium pastiche (blueprint grids, risograph grain, phosphor terminal green, arcade candy stripes).
- **Audio:** Crisp arcade chimes, bleeps, and snare hits synced to rapid microgame phases.
