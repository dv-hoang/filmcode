# Rubber Hose Animation - Source Architecture

- **Canvas 2D**: 1920x1080 resolution, hand-inked vintage parchment background (`#fbf9f1`), heavy black ink strokes (`#121212`).
- **Rubber-Hose Mechanics**: Equal-width tube limbs without elbows or knees, four-finger white gloves, pie eyes with wedge cutouts, and stretchy bouncing squash-and-stretch physics.
- **Audio**: Ragtime jazz accompaniment with snappy drums, hi-hats, brass stabs, and retro slide whistles generated via NumPy and SciPy.
