# One Ember — Treatment

**Style:** Woodcut Print (`woodcut`)
**Length:** 38.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps
**Language:** English · Kokoro `af_heart`
**Brief:** Original showcase story. No storyboard. Straight to production.

The library demo is a bell founder, a valley, and a failed casting. This film is one kiln block, recut. No bell, no valley, no snow, no sacrifice, no end-card sign-off.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Book of hours** — seven locked plates, one per watch of a night shift. *Rejected:* even pacing, and the first structure the style suggests.
2. **Two blocks in turn** — the kiln and the road outside, printed until one sheet carries both. *Rejected:* two sets in 38 s, and it leans toward the demo's valley cut.
3. **The same block recut** — one workshop block, carved deeper: grain becomes a kiln mouth, an ember, a feed, a flood of copper, then a jar. *Chosen:* one set, carving is the clock, the colour has somewhere to withdraw, the last corner can stay uncut.

**Opening:** the inked block itself. Horizontal grain, then the knife follows it until the grain is a kiln arch. Hard cut to the impression. Not a horizon line.
**Ending:** an uncarved corner of the same block, copper shrunk to the jar's mouth, letterpress title in the margin. Not a bell, not a last horizon cut.
**Score shape:** 72 BPM dorian drone and sparse spiccato, a digital silence, then 108 BPM under the copper (3:2 against 72), a second near-silence, a small metal strike, then a ritard to 54 over the uncut corner. No bell timbre. The loudest sound is a tam-tam.

---

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** A salt potter opens a kiln that died in the night and finds one ember; she feeds it until copper light is carved across the block, then the colour withdraws into a single jar, and one corner of the wood stays uncut.

**Arc:**
- **Setup (0–12 s):** The block is cut into a cold kiln. One ember is the only copper.
- **Turn (12–25 s):** She feeds it. After a silence, radial cuts burst and the colour plate spreads, then starts toward the jar.
- **Ending (25–38 s):** A silent snap to the mouth, one metal note, the stamp that read backwards prints true, and the knife never reaches the last corner.

---

## 8. Type

- Captions: Cormorant Garamond (the impasto OFL face; old-style letterpress), 42 px, ink, bottom margin only. In 0.10 s, out 0.15 s. No slide.
- The stamp `ASH` is cut into the jar. On the block it is mirrored; after the peel it reads true.
- Title `ONE EMBER` is letterpress in the margin from 33.55 to 38.00 (4.45 s), not a carved end card.

---

## 9. Colour

One plate: burnt copper `#C8502A`. It sits only in cuts. It starts as the ember, spreads at 19.70, travels into the jar, and ends as that mouth. Skin is protected from the plate so the face stays paper-white with black features.
