# 80s Cel Anime

An original 10-second Japanese OVA science-fiction sequence created entirely in code, capturing the golden age of 1980s cel animation aesthetics.

## Technical Details
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9)
- **Framerate**: 60 FPS
- **Techniques**:
  - Airbrushed gradient sunset backgrounds with parallax scrolling skyline and glowing anime window lights.
  - Hard-edged cel-shaded pilot silhouette, cockpit console, and backlit cyan/orange rim lighting.
  - Retro CRT scanlines and VHS tape vignette framing.
  - Synth-pop arpeggio soundtrack and cockpit alarm sound effects generated programmatically via Python.
