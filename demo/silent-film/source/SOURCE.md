# The Clockwork Professor & The Escaped Duck

A 1920s silent film photoplay created entirely in code, projected in an atmospheric picture house with red velvet curtains, hand-cranked jitter, sepia silver wash, vertical scratches, and an authentic honky-tonk photoplay piano soundtrack.

## Technical Details
- **Style:** 1920s Silent Film (Buster Keaton / Harold Lloyd era photoplay)
- **Visuals:** Canvas 2D, custom vector engraved-illustration style, stochastic film grain & vertical scratch simulation.
- **Audio:** Custom Python synthesis using NumPy and Soundfile (projector clatter, mechanical springs, honky-tonk piano chase cue).
