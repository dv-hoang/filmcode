# One Lamp Across

Shadow Puppetry. 39.0 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English. Storyteller: Kokoro `af_heart`.

A ferryman keeps one oil lamp. A child waits on the far bank with a dark lantern. Halfway, the flame gutters. Behind the screen, a hand cups it until it catches. The child's lantern answers, and the two lights walk the cloth dark on the way home.

## 1. Three structures

1. **A shore of lamps.** One figure walks a bank and lights a lamp at each post. Rejected: it is the obvious lantern-keeper procession, and the turn is only "more light."
2. **A face being opened.** A carver cuts a solid face into an open one, and the puppet then bows. Strong craft story, but the want is the carving, not a person reaching someone.
3. **A crossing.** One lamp, one river, one failure, one answer. Chosen. The want is the far bank. The turn is a flame that almost dies. The ending is the same seats as the opening, with the light gone home.

Opening image: the show is already in motion, one lamp on the near boat, a small figure on the far bank. Ending: pull back to those seats as both lamps leave with the figures and the cloth darkens from the side they exit. Score: sparse pentatonic fiddle and half-time drum, a hard silence, one suona phrase at the second lamp, then a flute and a last small gong.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline.** A ferryman's single lamp gutters halfway across a night river; a hand behind the screen saves the flame, a child's lantern answers, and two lights find the way home.

**Setup.** One lamp is already crossing. A child stands on the far bank with an unlit lantern.
**Turn.** The flame shrinks to almost nothing. The view trucks behind the cloth, where a hand hides the flame, and it catches.
**Ending.** The second lamp flares. They walk off the near side, the river strip lifts, and the light leaves with them.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn profile acting, lace openwork, rivets, lamp-count as brightness, and gong-and-drum phrasing. Do not reproduce a historical puppet, a named play, or a recorded melody. The on-screen title is the carved plaque `ONE LAMP`. No maker sign-off.
