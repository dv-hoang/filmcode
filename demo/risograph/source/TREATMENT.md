# The Crossing — treatment

Risograph Print. Original story. English. Kokoro `af_heart`. No storyboard. 40.0 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Two spot inks on warm paper: teal `#00838A` (plate 1, the night) and fluorescent pink `#FF48B0` (plate 2, the mail). The third colour is only their overprint.

## 1. Three structures

1. **Two inks in dialogue.** A teal night ferry and a pink mail boat alternate presence. They share a frame only late, and the water where they meet is the third colour. Opening: halftone dots pull back into the sea. Ending: the whole shot is a sheet pinned on a wall.
2. **Subtraction.** A full-colour harbour loses a plate at each departure and ends as one ink. The feeling is loss, and this story is a meeting.
3. **A zine.** Four turned pages of a harbour gazette. Page-turns would spend the forty seconds on paper mechanics and hide the boats.

**Chosen: 1.** The point of the film is the moment two inks make a path. The first idea for this style (a workshop tour of drums and swatches) is the cliché, so the film is a coast, not a shop.

Opening image: extreme close-up of teal halftone that pulls back until a bow cuts the dots. Ending: the harbour freezes into a poster, a hand pins it, the title double-hits. Score: bass alone with the night plate, marimba arrives with the pink plate, both together only at the crossing, then one nylon note.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** A teal night ferry waits on a paper coast for a pink mail boat; where their wakes cross, a third colour opens the path home.

- **Setup.** The night boat is already printed. She crosses an empty harbour. A paper disc sits in the sky, uninked.
- **Turn.** The pink plate roller-sweeps in with the mail boat. The two crews look across a gap and do not touch.
- **Ending.** Their wakes cross. The overprint is a path. The picture pulls back: it was one sheet, and a hand pins it up.

One subject (the ferry woman), one goal (the other boat), one turn (the wakes meet). Native move, at the peak: **two inks, a third colour where they meet.** The paper disc is a knockout that the pink plate fills. Registration drifts through the wait and snaps when the mail arrives; a kick hits when the wakes cross.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Tom Haugomat’s risograph illustrations** — learn: one horizon, one disc, one small figure; colour is a flat field; overprint is a real third hue. Do not take: his people, his towns, his palette, any poster layout.
- **Jacques Tati, *Jour de fête*** — learn: a small body in a wide frame; comedy of scale; everyday machine sounds as rhythm. Do not take: the postman, the fair, the gags, the music.

## 4. Shot list

Camera moves on ones. Characters, boats and the flag step on twos. Cuts on the 96 BPM grid (beat = 0.625 s).

| Shot | Time | Framing | Angle / move | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–5.0 | Close, then full | Pull back out of the dots | Hook: the dots are the sea, and a bow enters before 3 s. Entering the print. |
| 2 | 5.0–10.0 | Wide | Lateral track with the ferry | Travel and routine. Small figure, empty sky. |
| 3 | 10.0–15.0 | Wide, locked | Static. Horizon, paper disc, ferry | The breath. She waits. Registration drifts, then snaps on the downbeat. |
| 4 | 15.0–20.0 | Full, big bow | Low angle, slight rise as the boat settles | The second plate arrives. Energy. A roller sweep is the cut. |
| 5 | 20.0–27.5 | Medium two-shot | Locked, then a small hold | Reaction. They look. The gap stays paper and teal. |
| 6 | 27.5–32.5 | Full on the water | Push into the crossing | Signature. The pink wake grows over the teal sea. The path is the subject and fills the lower frame. Kick on the downbeat. |
| 7 | 32.5–40.0 | The sheet on a wall | Pull back | It was one print. A hand pins the corner. Title double-hit. |

Four moves at least: pull, track, low-angle rise, push, pull-back. The two-shot is the held pause. Pace: the track is the quicker passage; shot 3 and the look are slow; the wake grows in about 1.4 s; the ending decelerates into the pin.

## 8. Type

Printed, not overlaid. Titles and subtitles are drawn into the plate canvas so they misregister and grain with the ink.

- **Title:** “THE CROSSING”, Cormorant Garamond 700 (the heavy weight shipped with the Impasto fonts; the style’s grotesque is not in that folder). Two plates, visible offset, roller wipe starting 35.8, fully on by 36.15, held to 40.0.
- **Subtitles:** Cormorant Garamond 600, about 44 px, one plate (teal), on a paper-white knockout strip. Bottom of frame, except the two-shot and the crossing, which sit at the top so they do not cover the faces or the path. Hold is at least 1.8 s, the spoken length plus 0.6 s, and letters/15 + 1.5 s. Hard cut, no fade.
- **Voice:** six short lines, `af_heart`, relaxed. “Home.” is the last word, before the title, not over it.
