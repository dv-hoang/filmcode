# First Orange — Treatment

**Style:** Impasto Oil Painting (`impasto`)  
**Length:** ~42 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps  
**Language:** English (Kokoro offline)  
**Brief:** Original showcase story; go straight to production.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One object, many hands** — a ceramic bowl passes potter → market → table; colour arrives only when it holds breakfast. *Rejected:* too many sets for ~40 s; dilutes the knife-paint focus.
2. **Portrait in layers** — a face painted coarse→fine as a life is told. *Rejected:* needs character performance beyond this brief's scope.
3. **Still life comes alive** — locked farm table at grey dawn; one orange is the only chroma; a knife peels it and colour re-lays the kitchen stroke by stroke. *Chosen:* one set, native colour-reveal, readable silhouettes, strong subject for the medium.

**Chosen opening:** mid-grey still life already on the table (no slow logo).  
**Chosen ending:** pull-back to the finished plate hanging as morning light; one last scrape lands the title.  
**Score shape:** sparse solo cello → pizzicato climb on peel beats → warm ensemble swell as colour floods → soft resolve.

---

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** In a grey dawn kitchen, one orange waits; when the knife breaks its skin, colour floods the room stroke by stroke — and breakfast begins.

**Arc:**
- **Setup (0–10 s):** Grey kitchen table, cool umber shadows. The orange alone holds chroma. Narration: waiting.
- **Turn (10–24 s):** A knife enters; peel strips fall as knife-dabs; juice drips paint colour into the cloth; colour-reveal wave sweeps the room.
- **Ending (24–42 s):** Warm light rakes the ridges; a second orange appears in a bowl; title laid by the knife; soft hold.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo:** 72 BPM (sections A–B), 96 BPM (peel climb), 72 BPM resolve.  
- **Grid:** 1 beat = 60/72 ≈ 0.833 s (slow); peel section 60/96 = 0.625 s.  
- **Hits:** knife contact @ beat; each peel land on beat; reveal start on downbeat; title on downbeat; "Breakfast." on downbeat after silence.
