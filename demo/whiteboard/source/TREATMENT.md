# The Open Top — treatment

Whiteboard Explainer. Original film. English. Kokoro `af_heart`. No storyboard.
1920×1080, 24 fps, 40.0 s (16 bars at 96 BPM). Loudness −14 LUFS.

Not the library demo and not the 10 s card “The Garden That Waited”.

## 1. Three structures

**A. A proof, downward.** Claim “a lid keeps tea hot” boxed at the top; each step appears under it and gets a box when spoken. Clear, and the first shape this style suggests. Rejected: it reads as a worksheet, and the camera only scrolls down.

**B. One cooling curve.** A long horizontal time axis, temperature hanging off it, camera tracking left to right. Good for “sequence”, weak for the wrong idea the film has to undo. The eraser would only wipe a wiggle in the line.

**C. Two mugs, one argument.** Left half: an open mug, heat leaving the surface. Centre: a ghost lesson that blames the ceramic, wiped backwards. Right half: the same mug with a lid, the same heat trapped. Two pens draw the two temperature lines until they can be seen together. Chosen. The board is a map of the argument (open → myth → lid), the eraser is the turn, and the full-board view is the summary.

**Opening image.** An orange magnet — one packet of heat — drops onto an almost empty board and clicks. The title is not first; the drop is.

**Ending.** The camera pushes into the lid’s small circle. The pen circles the word “lid” twice and caps. The open mug is still visible, smaller, at the left edge: the bookend.

**Score shape.** Plucked nylon guitar and kalimba, not a marimba clock. One four-note kalimba cell on the grid. The guitar doubles it from bar 3. Both stop dead in bar 7. After the wipe they return as a still dyad (the heat that stays), with no second tempo and no reversed cue. A single woodblock marks the unison at bar 13 only. Silence in bar 7, before the eraser.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline.** Heat leaves a mug through the open top; a lid closes that door, and the same heat stays.

**Setup.** One packet of heat sits in an open mug and walks out across the surface. The wall is drawn as the slow door.

**Turn.** A ghost of an old lesson still says the cup is cold, so the tea is cold. The room goes quiet. The eraser wipes that sentence backwards. The first sound after the quiet is the felt rub, then the magnet’s click as the lid shuts.

**Ending.** The same magnet tries the top, cannot leave, and sinks back into the tea. Both mugs are on the board at once. The word “lid” is circled, and the pen caps.

## 3. Benchmark

**Learn from.** minutephysics: one sentence, one drawing, the simplest geometry that carries the idea (a U for a mug, an arrow for a path, a falling line for temperature). RSA Animate / Cognitive Media: one continuous board, the camera travels, a full-board view at the end. Steve Reich, *Piano Phase*: two copies of one figure at two tempi while the mugs disagree, unison when the argument resolves. Grammar only.

**Do not take.** Any existing drawing of a mug, any hand, any on-screen presenter, any Reich melody or recording, any brand of mug or tea, the library demo’s story, regions, props or timeline.

Foley sits about 11 dB under the voice. Voice is compressed and rides above the music. Two real silences: the end of bar 7, and the beat before the eraser squeak in bar 8.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

- Board text is single-stroke handwriting, written in stroke order. Title “THE OPEN TOP” at large cap height (~150 px on the board). Labels “slow door” and “lid” at ~48 px. The word finishes as it is spoken.
- Burned subtitles: off-white rounded label at ~90% opacity, Cormorant Garamond italic ~44 px (the film’s serif, from the shared demo font folder; Architects Daughter is not in that folder), dark navy text, a short orange marker dash at the left of the label. Bottom ~12% kept clear. Split at clauses, each line ≤ 44 characters.
- Cues (holds ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s); the short last line is held to the end of the film):

## Colour

Three markers, fixed meanings:

- Navy `#1f2a44` — things and structure: mug, title, labels, frame of the graphs.
- Teal `#1f8a8a` — measurement and process: arrows, the falling temperature, the slow-door ticks.
- Orange `#d97757` — the one thing that matters: the heat magnet, the flat temperature line, the subtitle dash, that marker’s cap.

Ghost marks stay grey-blue at about 7%. The orange never appears on a prop except the heat magnet and the orange marker’s cap.
