# Projected Model

A pure white architectural massing model stands on a dark wooden plinth in a gallery room. An overhead digital projector paints successive layers of data—topography, solar shadows, and floorplate density—directly onto the white plaster surfaces.

## Aesthetic & Technical Notes
- **Style**: Projected White Model (`projected-model`).
- **Visuals**: Canvas 2D isometric projection rendering realistic plaster building massing, procedural lighting, and editorial data graphics overlays.
- **Audio**: Ambient projector cooling fan hum paired with clean analog layer-switch chimes and precise stepper relay clicks.
