# Microscope

An optical bio-pathology immersion film depicting a continuous 1000x zoom dive from a glass slide stage down to cellular structures, complete with live instrumentation HUD overlays, focal plane breathing, and motorized focus servo audio.

## Aesthetic & Grammar
- **Source Material**: Procedurally generated organic whole-slide tissue specimen (H&E stain aesthetic).
- **Camera Movement**: Continuous exponential zoom with fluid mechanical stage drift.
- **HUD & Readouts**: Real-time numerical magnification, scale bar (µm), numerical aperture, coordinates, and LED illumination status.
- **Audio**: Low-frequency cooling fan rumble, objective lens servo sweep tones, stepper motor clicks, and system status chimes.
