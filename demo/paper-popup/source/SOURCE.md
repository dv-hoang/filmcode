# The Clockwork Aviary — Paper Pop-up Book

A 10-second macro-photography styled paper pop-up book scene featuring layered V-folds, thick white-bordered paper cutouts, golden rotating brass gears, and a mechanical bird springing to life.

- **Engine**: FilmCode Canvas 2D / WebGL-styled paper dioramas with macro depth of field vignette.
- **Audio**: Custom synthesized paper rustles, spring chimes, and mechanical clockwork gear clicks.
