# The Night Lamp — treatment

Copperplate engraving, 38.0 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English. Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`. No storyboard. Invented plate: no foundry, no real species, no real coast.

## Structures

1. **Three states of one plate.** A shut seed is engraved, a window is burnished out of the valve, and the same plate is re-cut open so a wick can take a single amber wash. Chosen: the turn is something only intaglio can do, and the ending can set the true-size seed beside the enlargement.
2. **A book of four small plates** (seed, valve, wick, maker), one fact each. Clear, but no turn; it becomes a catalogue.
3. **A teaching dissection** that labels every part and colours the lesson. The wick would be a diagram, not a reveal.

Opening image: the burin already in a groove, raking light on bare copper, the upper shoulder of the shell partly cut. Ending: the finished plate, wick amber, and the uncoloured seed at natural size in the margin. Score: dry harpsichord while the burin works; two real silences; viola only when the wash arrives.

## Logline and arc

An engraver enlarges a shut night-lamp seed, burnishes the plate open, and lays one amber wash on the wick — then sets the seed itself, true size, beside the proof.

Setup: the shell is only a seam and a contour. Turn: the seam is burnished away and cut open. Ending: colour stays on the wick; the real seed, in the margin, is still shut and still bare.

## Benchmark

Audubon’s elephant-folio plates and a Doré burin, as grammar only. Learn: one specimen centred, italic name under roman capitals, a numbered detail flown to the margin, hatching that follows the form, light from one side. Do not take: any bird, any Doré scene, any real border, any foundry, any published binomial.

## Type and subtitles

Plate lettering is Cormorant Garamond (the film’s roman; italics for the name and labels; italic script for the note). Roman glyphs cut one by one. The note writes left to right. Nothing fades.

- THE NIGHT LAMP — roman capitals, ~50 px, wide tracking, from 10.70.
- *Lumenconcha vespera* — italic, ~23 px, from 12.40. Spoken nowhere (the checker never hears it).
- PLATE IV, *hinge*, *valve*, *wick*, *natural size*, *I. Marrow del. et sculp.*
- Subtitles: one deckled slip, bottom centre, tapped down and peeled off. No fade. Holds below are longer than speech + 0.6 s and at least 1.8 s.

## What this is not

Not the library’s bell-foundry card. Different subject, opening (raking copper, not a finished bell), structure (three states of one plate), signature (burnish, then a roundel, then one wash), score (harpsichord into viola), and ending (true-size seed).
