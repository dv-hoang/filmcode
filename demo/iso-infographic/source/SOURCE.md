# Global Carbon Cycle — Isometric Infographic

An animated isometric infographic illustrating the global carbon cycle, anthropogenic emissions, biospheric sinks, and net atmospheric accumulation over a 10-second sequence.

- **Style**: Isometric Infographic (Kurzgesagt / SimCity 2000 cutaway diorama style)
- **Palette**: Deep slate blue sky, layered soil strata, clean green foliage and warm industrial amber/red accents.
- **Audio**: Atmospheric pad, interface click cues, and subtle harmonic chimes on data revelation.
