# First Bloom — treatment

Paper-cut Lightbox. Original story. English. Kokoro `af_heart`. No storyboard. 39.0 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, on ones (every frame is a new picture; the brief locks 24 fps).

One subject: a paper potting-shed lamp. One goal: get a single night-blooming camellia to open. One turn: the match does not light the flower; the light has to find the glass first, and only then does the flower write itself out of red card. Ending: the camera leaves the box and the lamp is a small object on a table, still on.

## 1. Three structures

**A. One window, four seasons.** A locked frame; only the light and the plant change. Rejected: four similar holds, no turn you can see in one glance, and the first idea this style suggests.

**B. A coin down a well.** Tilt from the moon through eaves to a coin at the bottom. Rejected: the action is a fall with nothing that changes its mind; the ending is a sparkle, not an echo.

**C. The match, the glass, the flower, then the table.** Chosen. Darkness and one hole (the moon) in the first three seconds. The match is the native light-burst. The windows are the native “lamp turns on,” saved for the spoken word. The flower is paper that grows from its stem, the only red in the film. The ending pulls out of the box so the same light is suddenly spill on a cup and a pair of shears. One place, one ladder, one object that turns out to be a keepsake.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** In a paper potting shed that has gone dark, one match is struck; the light finds the glass, a single red camellia opens, and the camera steps back to the lamp left burning on a table.

**Setup:** The shed is only stacked card. A moon-hole is the only light.
**Turn:** The match wakes the paper, but the flower waits until the windows burn.
**Ending:** Pull out of the box. The lamp sits on a wooden table. Its spill hits a clay cup and a pair of shears. A cut-paper title, FIRST BLOOM, grows on the front band. The spoken line is different: “She left the lamp on.”

## 3. Benchmark

Learn from the style, not from any existing film: layered card, backlight, cast shadows, holes that bloom, paper that grows from an edge, a pull-out that admits the box is an object.

Do not learn a route, a vehicle, a tunnel, name boards, a ticket, or a windows-on / windows-off mirror. This film has no journey and no second place. The score is a music box, a koto and a kalimba — not a travelling ostinato, and not one chime per window.

Compared with the library demo after this treatment was planned: different structure (one shed, not a trip), different opening (a moon-hole, not a halt), different signature (a flower that writes itself, then the box on a table), different camera (dolly, rack, tilt, pull-out of the box — no travelling alongside), different score shape (sparse box motif, two silences, a kalimba entrance), different ending (keepsake table, not a terminus).

## 8. Type

Latin serif: Cormorant Garamond, from the impasto demo’s OFL files.

**Paper title:** FIRST BLOOM, cut as light through the front band, growing from the left edge between 34.0 and 35.0, held to 39.0. Not subtitled.

**Subtitles:** italic Cormorant, 38 px, warm off-white `#f4efe4`, soft shadow. Full-bleed shots: 64 px from the bottom, faint dark band. From 33.2 s (box framing): 74 px from the bottom, on the dark room, band removed. No wrap. Each cue ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).

## Camera notes

fov 26°. Full-bleed camera stays near z 0.21–0.48 so the sheet fills the frame. The pull continues to about z 0.88 so the wooden frame is fully inside the picture with the table under it. Hand-held drift ±0.6 mm on every frame, including the locked match.
