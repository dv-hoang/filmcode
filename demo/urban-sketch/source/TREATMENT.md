# Still Lit — treatment

**Style:** Urban Sketch · Pen & Wash (`styles/urban-sketch/STYLE.md`) · **Length:** 40.0 s · **1920×1080 @ 24 fps** · **English**, Kokoro `af_heart`
**Topic:** an invented café corner, Pigeon & Third. No real street, shop, or person.

Compared with the library demo *Where the Wind Went* (a straw hat carried across a park): this film does not chase an object, does not open on a coloured prop, does not follow with a spring, does not use a jazz waltz, and does not end on a travel title. The wash arrives as the afternoon, then leaves. One window keeps it.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. A spool unspools down a lane.** One coloured thing pays out and paints what it touches. Strength: a follow-shot. Weakness: it is the same machine as a hat stolen by the wind. Dropped after reading the demo.

**B. Five diary pages.** The wind turns the sheet. Weakness: forty seconds cannot give five days a turn.

**C. One corner, the wash is the clock.** The ink is drawn once. Colour arrives from the upper left as the afternoon, the two figures move only after it reaches them, then the pigment drains away and a single window stays amber.

**Chosen: C.** Opening image: a blank margin, a handwritten time, the first line of the street. Ending: the same kind of note, now 7:40, under a window that is the only colour left on the sheet.

## 2. Logline and arc

> On an invented corner, a fineliner café is drawn at four in the afternoon; the wash is the day leaving, and when the chairs are empty only one window is still lit.

- **Setup (0–9 s):** The pen draws the corner. The only marks are sepia. A date sits in the sky margin.
- **Turn (9–27 s):** Colour blooms from the upper left. The waiter pours, the sitter drinks, then stands. The chairs empty. The wash begins to recede toward the façade.
- **Ending (27–40 s):** The corner returns to ink. At the moment the camera arrives on the upper window, a lamp blooms and stays. The margin reads *Still Lit*, *Thursday, 7:40*, *Pigeon & Third*.

## 3. Benchmark

**Learn from:** on-location sketchbook pages (line, then a misregistered wash, a date in the margin, the sheet fraying into dabs); a held frame before a change; solo nylon guitar as a café instrument.
**Do not take:** the demo's hat, park, chase, waltz, skyline, or end card. No real café, sign, or melody.

## 6. Cue map

- 80 BPM, 4/4. Beat = 0.75 s. Bar = 3.0 s. Bars at 0, 3, 6 … 39, then a one-second hold.
- Brush on the downbeat of the bar at 9.0 (just after). Chair on the beat at 19.5 area (19.4). Lamp on the arrival of the push, 30.45. Last pen-stroke finishes by 37.6 so the lines hold.
- **Instruments:** nylon guitar only as the melodic instrument (fingered bass notes and a small upper voice), upright piano on four soft chords and once under the lamp. No clarinet, no walking jazz bass, no brushes-as-kit, no strings.
- **Process:** sparse guitar (0–7.5) → silence → guitar plus two piano notes as the wash spreads (9–17.6) → silence → guitar returns thinner as the chairs empty → from 27 only quarter notes → one piano colour at 30.5 → a last guitar note at 36.75 that rings under the margin.
- Voice, not on the silences:
  - 1.30– "Thursday. The corner was only a line."
  - 9.60– "Then the afternoon sat down."
  - 21.30– "The chairs are empty."
  - 31.15– "One window kept the light."
- Each line holds at least the speech plus 0.6 s. The end title holds at least 4 s.

## 8. Subtitle and title design

All lettering is Caveat, sepia, written left to right.

- Corner note, sky margin, on the page: "Thursday, 4:10". It leaves with the camera.
- Narration is a caption strip in the lower margin of the frame: Caveat, no box, no plate.
- End, in the blank margin under the empty tables, on the page: "Still Lit", "Thursday, 7:40", "Pigeon & Third".

Ink is one sepia `#3f332c`. Paper `#f4ecdf` is the light, including the middle of the lit pane. Afternoon wash: stone `#e4d2b4`, shadow mixed 45% toward `#7a7396`, awning `#9c3a4a`, leaves `#5f8a45`, distant blocks mixed toward `#c5cdd4`, sky a few peach ellipses `#e8ba96`. The lamp is `#e4b14e`, and it does not drain.
