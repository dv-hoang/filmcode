# The Rose Window

A 10-second stained glass cathedral vignette exploring light, sacred geometry, and medieval glasscraft.

## Concept & Visuals
- **Style**: Stained-Glass (`stained-glass`)
- **Composition**: A massive Gothic rose window and lancet arches set into dark stone masonry.
- **Lighting**: Dawn sunlight sweeping through hand-blown ruby, sapphire, and amber glass panels, projecting chromatic light onto the cold stone floor below.
- **Animation**: Young Sherlock Holmes-inspired glass fracture and reassembly mechanics accompanied by floating golden dust motes.

## Technical Details
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Canvas 2D)
- **Duration**: 10 seconds (Deterministic rendering via `draw(t)`)
- **Audio**: Custom Python synthesis featuring bronze cathedral bells, crystalline glass shards, and atmospheric stone-hall wind.
